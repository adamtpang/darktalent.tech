# OUTREACH: darktalent.tech

**DRAFTS ONLY. Nothing here has been sent. Adam sends.**

Target: a founder or hiring manager at a company with a technical req open right
now. The signal that they are a live target is a job posting older than about
three weeks, because that means the seat is not filling itself.

Rules that shape these drafts:
- Lead with the artifact, not the pitch. Send the shortlist logic, not adjectives.
- Never promise a candidate we cannot produce. The pool is small and honest.
- The price is drafted and unapproved, so no draft below quotes a number.
  Once Adam approves The Shortlist at $500, add it to variant A.

## Variant A: cold, they have a public posting

Subject: five candidates for your {ROLE} req

Hi {NAME},

Your {ROLE} posting has been up about {N} weeks, which usually means the
applicant pile is big and the signal in it is low.

I run darktalent. We score builders on demonstrated public output, things like
what they have shipped, whether other people depend on it, and whether their
output is accelerating. Pedigree counts as a discount rather than a credit,
because the market already overpays for it.

Send me the JD and I will come back with five candidates ranked for that
specific seat, each with the evidence behind the score. If none of them are
worth a first interview, you have lost one email.

Worth a look?

{ADAM}
darktalent.tech

## What to do when they reply

1. Get the real JD. Paste it into /hiring to get the parsed seat and the first
   pass. Confirm the seat reading with them before promising anything.
2. Be honest about pool depth. Today the pool is small and seeded with
   composites, so a real req may need sourcing before the shortlist is real.
   Say that rather than shipping five weak names.
3. Do not discount to win the work. Per the Moneyball read, open contingency is
   an auction decided by whoever has the most recruiter hours, and that is the
   one contest this business cannot win.

## Do not send to

Network School members or anyone in the supply side. They are supply, they only
receive value, and they never pay. See `ECOSYSTEM.md`.
